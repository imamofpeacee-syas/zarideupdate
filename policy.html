Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • Version: 1.2

1. Overview

At Zaride, your privacy and security are paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application, website, and ride-hailing services.

By using Zaride, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide fast, reliable, and safe transportation services:

  • Account Data: Full name, phone number, email address, and profile picture.
  • Location Data: Real-time precise location from your device when using trip features.
  • Transaction Details: Ride history, payment receipts, and payment method details (processed securely via encrypted gateways).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized strictly to deliver and improve our services:

Core Operations: Matching you with nearby driver partners, calculating upfront fares, and providing live GPS trip navigation.
  • To verify identity and prevent fraudulent account activity.
  • To process seamless in-app payments and deliver trip receipts.
  • To offer 24/7 customer support and emergency response features.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data. We only share minimal necessary information in the following situations:

  • With Drivers: Pickup and dropoff locations, first name, and phone mask (for call/chat coordination).
  • For Legal Requirements: When required by court orders or authorized government requests.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You maintain full control over your personal information:

  • Access & Edit: Update your profile anytime from the app settings.
  • Data Deletion: Request account closure and permanent removal of personal records.
  • Location Permissions: Toggle device location permissions in system settings at any time.
