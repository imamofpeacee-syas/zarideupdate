About Zaride

Origin Story

This initiative was produced out of a well thought, and thorough investigation on the transportation system in Zaria. While local transit appeared cheap, residents still faced significant challenges including safety concerns, erratic overcharging, and uncomfortable transit conditions. Zaride was built to solve these exact gaps through structure, technology, and trust.

The Core Identity

Mission Statement

To provide the residents of Zaria with a smoother, more developed, professional, and efficient mode of transportation. Furthermore, to offer car owners an empowered way to build wealth by onboarded transit opportunities.

Vision Statement

Seeing Zaria transform from being a virgin landscape into a thriving hub of opportunity, modern infrastructure, and economic development.
